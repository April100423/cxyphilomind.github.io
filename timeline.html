IDEAS ACROSS TIME

A Timeline of Philosophy

Philosophy developed across many regions and traditions. These broad periods are a guide, not a claim that every tradition follows the same timeline.

c. 600 BCE – 500 CE

Ancient traditions

Greek philosophy explored virtue, knowledge, and reality; Chinese traditions developed rich debates about ethics, governance, and the Dao.

SocratesPlatoAristotleConfuciusLaoziZhuangzi
c. 500 – 1500

Medieval philosophy

Philosophers across Islamic, Jewish, Christian, South Asian, and East Asian contexts debated reason, faith, logic, and reality.

AvicennaAl-GhazaliMaimonidesAquinas
c. 1500 – 1800

Early modern philosophy

Scientific change prompted new questions about knowledge, mind, political authority, and the foundations of certainty.

DescartesLockeSpinozaHumeKant
1800 – early 20th century

Modern movements

Industrialization and political change shaped debates about history, freedom, value, society, and logic.

MarxMillKierkegaardNietzscheRussell
20th century to today

Contemporary philosophy

Philosophers developed new approaches to language, gender, race, mind, science, technology, and global justice.

Arendtde BeauvoirWittgensteinButlerNussbaum
