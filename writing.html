Philosophy Writing Studio

A philosophy essay does more than state an opinion. It makes a claim, supports it with reasons, and responds fairly to serious objections.

Argument builder

Build a strong argument

  1. Define: clarify terms that could be ambiguous.
  2. Claim: answer the question directly.
  3. Reason: show how your premises support the conclusion.
  4. Objection: address the strongest challenge, not the easiest one.
  5. Revision: explain what your argument can and cannot establish.
